## How to reach out

Message me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aleksandar-grbic-74670263/). A short note is enough:

- What you are building and who it is for
- When you want to be live (or when you needed to be live yesterday)
- Whether you have a technical co-founder or need me to own the whole path to production

I reply to every serious inquiry. Engagements are scoped and billed separately. BoringStack itself stays free and open source; you are paying for my time and judgment, not a license.
